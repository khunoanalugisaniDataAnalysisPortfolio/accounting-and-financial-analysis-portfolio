Project Components

1. Artificial Intelligence (AI) in Business

Researched and critically evaluated the application of Artificial Intelligence within Discovery Group Limited.

Key Focus Areas:

Identification and evaluation of AI applications.

Benchmarking AI adoption against competitors and industry leaders.

AI governance, ethics and responsible implementation.

Benefits and risks of AI in financial reporting and auditing.

Recommendations for improving AI adoption.

2. Five-Year Financial Performance Analysis

Conducted a financial analysis of the company's Statement of Profit or Loss over a five-year period (FY2021–FY2025).

Key Focus Areas:

Analysis of net profit trends.

Identification of significant financial variances and outliers.

Evaluation of factors influencing changes in profitability.

Application of Excel formulas and PivotTables.

Use of conditional formatting to highlight financial trends, anomalies and variances.

Tools and Technologies

Microsoft Excel

PivotTables

Excel Formulas

Conditional Formatting

Financial Statement Analysis

AI Research and Evaluation

Project Objective

To demonstrate how digital technologies and analytical tools can enhance financial analysis, support business decision-making and contribute to the evolving role of accounting professionals.

Project Area: Professional Values, Attitudes and Acumens (PVAA) – Digital Acumen
